# Review: live chats, Oct 30 02:00 → Oct 31 01:30 (23 h)

46 chats had activity: 282 customer messages, 274 AI text messages, **10 AI orders** (#68–#77), 107 handoffs
in the logs over the period. No crashes; only a few Telegram network blips. Customers are named here only by
order number or a short description.

## What works
- Libyan dialect is clean: no سعرو / وش / عشان in the AI's messages.
- No "من أي بلد" to Libyan numbers. Arabic only (Turkish ad openings were answered in Arabic).
- Size by weight works (95 kg → 60, 97 kg → 60, 80 kg → 56), sold-out colours and sizes are offered alternatives.
- 👍 on the summary asks "نثبّت استاذ؟" and waits for words (#75, #77).
- Bank details are copied exactly when the customer chooses a transfer (#74).
- Orders are mostly complete and correct: two sets as two items (#71), corrections followed (#68: one set, the
  number chosen).

## What went wrong

### 1. Too many "حاضر، لحظة نتأكدلك ونرجعلك 🙏" (23 times in 23 h)
The customer waits and the sale cools. Most were questions the live knowledge answers:
- "وين مكانكم / عنوان المحل / صالة عرض / وكيل في بنغازي" (5 chats).
- "فرصة أخرى" and "شكرا… مره تانية" (polite goodbyes) — handed off.
- "نطلب يوم الاثنين" / "next week" — handed off as a "later-date booking".
- A delivery-time question after the customer gave the city — handed off.
- A 👍 reaction on a photo message — handed off.
- "Can I transfer from التجارة والتنمية bank?" — the reply check scored 60 and blocked a correct answer.
- The size "62" plus colour "كريمي" — handed off with a reason that said "this handoff should not be used".
Handoff reasons in the logs say it outright: "Reviewer conflict", "not meant to be a handoff", "I was able to
respond". **Cause:** the code's instructions still say "if unsure hand off with لحظة نتأكدلك", "silent handoff
for the exact address", and the reply check hands off below 80; the team's live knowledge says the opposite. The
model obeys one, the checker flags it against the other, and it hands off.

### 2. Quantity handled by the old rule
- "الأبيض والفضي والكريمي" and "كل اللون" → "الفريق يتواصل معاك بخصوص الكمية" (the live knowledge now says take
  the order normally).
- "نبي تلات قطع كم التخفيض" was read as **3 sets**; the customer meant the 3-piece set. (A discount request is
  still a team case, but the reason and message were wrong.)

### 3. Answers that miss the question
- "Can I inspect the quality before receiving?" → the bot answered about weight and size.
- "رجعته من امس" (returned the order) → "نبدلوه بطقم كامل مقاس 60؟".
- A long loop on "طول": the bot asked the same clarification 3 times, then handed off.

### 4. Order data
- **#74**: notes say "أرسل إيصال بعد التحويل" — the customer never sent a receipt. Invented by the order extraction.
- **#72**: phone `901012457` (9 digits, not a valid Libyan mobile) was accepted: `intlPhone` takes any 9-digit
  number starting with 9. Libyan mobiles are 091/092/093/094/095.
- #76: "100 100" was taken as the confirmation (probably meant yes, but ambiguous).

### 5. Wording slips
- A man was called "استاذة" with his own (male) name: the chat had started with a woman's voice note, and the
  order was for her husband.
- "شن اللون اللي يعجبك واستاذ؟", "يوصلَك", "منطقةك" — small typos.
- "ما نقدر نوريك المقاسين بصور، المقاس رقم مو شكل" — "مو" is not Libyan.

### 6. Policy facts the team stated that the bot doesn't know
- Refusing at the door: the team told two customers they pay the delivery fee. The repo knowledge said "ما تدفع
  شي"; the live knowledge now covers it only for heavy customers. One rule is needed.
- "Biggest size fits up to 110 kg" (the team) vs the size table.

## Not problems
- Chats the team handled (delivery calls, courier numbers) — the bot stayed out, as it should with the 12 h pause.
- Two replies close together (#77: colour, then size) — the customer's second message came after the first reply
  was already sent; the stale-reply guard works.
